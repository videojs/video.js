We're excited to announce the Release Candidate of Video.js v10. The project has come a long way since [its tech preview 10 months ago](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dR4Wmj_BIsQ&t=4s&pp=ygUTdmlkZW8uanMgMTAgZGVtdXhlZA%3D%3D). Try it out at <GetStartedLink client:idle className={shared.a}>videojs.org/docs</GetStartedLink>

## What is version 10?

Video.js v10 is a ground-up rewrite of not only Video.js, but also of [Plyr](https://github.com/sampotts/plyr/discussions/2871), [Vidstack](https://github.com/vidstack/player/discussions/1747), and [Media Chrome](https://www.mux.com/blog/from-media-chrome-to-video-js-v10-the-evolution-of-html-first-video-players). It's a big shift in how web media players are built.

Why are we making this change? Like we said in our [beta blog post](/blog/videojs-v10-beta-hello-world-again), we're:

- Shrinking bundle sizes, and then shrinking them more (the default bundle is over 60% smaller than Video.js 8)
- Allowing deep customization using the familiar development patterns of your chosen framework — including new first-class React, Typescript, and Tailwind support
- Making the defaults look beautiful and perform beautifully
- Designing the codebase and docs so AI agents building your player alongside you can actually be good at it

Do [check that blog post out](/blog/videojs-v10-beta-hello-world-again); Steve did a great job of laying it all out.

### What's new since beta

We promised GA mid-2026. And if you _really squint…_ September is still closer to the middle of the year than the end… right?

It's taken a while because we put in a lot of work. Dozens of releases, hundreds of fixes and features, almost a thousand PRs. In that time, we've gone from a foundation to a mature player:

- SPF, our in-house engine composition framework, went from a spike to prod-ready, while still maintaining those svelte bundle sizes
- There are dozens of new UI components, from menus to hotkey/gesture input systems. Our preset skins support them all so they're easy to use out of the box
- We stressed and validated our architecture with tough features like live, internationalization, DRM, and the dreaded _Google Cast_
- The media component family got bigger. HLS and Mux have been joined by DASH, Shaka, Vimeo, YouTube, Cloudflare Stream, Wistia, Twitch, TikTok, Spotify, and more
- We let our API simmer and it's more flavorful than ever. After feedback and testing, we've made 34 breaking changes, each bringing us closer to that Just Works™ Developer Experience. And now, we're ready to call that API stable.
