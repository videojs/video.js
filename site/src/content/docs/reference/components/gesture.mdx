## Behavior

<FrameworkCase frameworks={["react"]}>
`Gesture` is a behavior component: it renders no visible interface. Place it inside a <DocsLink slug="reference/components/player-container">Container</DocsLink> to map a `tap` or `doubletap` gesture to a player action.
</FrameworkCase>

<FrameworkCase frameworks={["html"]}>
`<media-gesture>` is a behavior component: it renders no visible interface. Place it inside a <DocsLink slug="reference/components/player-container">`<media-container>`</DocsLink> to map a `tap` or `doubletap` gesture to a player action.
</FrameworkCase>

A tap must use the primary pointer and complete within 250 milliseconds. When a matching double-tap gesture exists, the first tap waits briefly to see whether a second tap follows.

Use `pointer` to accept only `mouse`, `touch`, or `pen` input. Use `region` to divide the container horizontally:

- Left and right regions split the container into halves.
- Left, center, and right regions split it into thirds.
- A center-only region covers the full container.
- A single left or right region covers that half of the container.

An unregional gesture acts as a fallback outside the active regions. When several registrations match the same gesture, the first registration wins.

Choose a built-in `action` from the API reference below, or use an application-defined name to call a same-named action on the player store. Pass `value` as seconds for `seekStep`, as a volume delta such as `0.05` or `-0.05` for `volumeStep`, or as a rate change such as `0.1` for `speedUp` and `speedDown`. When omitted, the seek and volume actions use the 10-second or five-percentage-point default, and the speed actions move to the next faster or slower of the player's playback rates. A left-region seek gesture uses the negative step. Unknown action names do nothing and warn in development.

## Accessibility

Gestures supplement visible, keyboard-operable controls; they should never be the only way to perform an action. Gestures ignore pointer activity that begins on interactive controls, links, form fields, menu items, or elements marked with `data-interactive`.
