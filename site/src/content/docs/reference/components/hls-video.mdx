HLS video component built on Video.js's own streaming engine, optimized for a small bundle. The <DocsLink slug="reference/components/hlsjs-video">hls.js video component</DocsLink> is the larger, more compatible alternative and takes hls.js engine options. The <DocsLink slug="reference/components/native-hls-video">native HLS video component</DocsLink> hands playback to browsers with built-in HLS. <DocsLink slug="guides/media-sources">Media sources</DocsLink> explains how `src` and `source` work.
