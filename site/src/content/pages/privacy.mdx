Video.js is a community-built, free and open source HTML5 video player framework. This Privacy Notice describes what information Video.js collects, and does not collect, in connection with its website and the free and open source HTML5 video player.

## Information We Collect and How We Use It

### Information Collected Through Our Website

Like most websites, our web servers automatically collect standard technical information when you interact with our website, including IP address, device type and version, referring website addresses, and the pages you have requested, events our servers observe, and the relevant timestamps for each activity.

This information is used solely for maintaining our website's functionality, security and performance, as well as to understand how individuals are using the website. This information is not used for purposes of profiling website visitors, tracking individuals across services or browsing sessions, or conducting advertising.

### Information Collected Through Our Community Channels and Development Platforms

We manage and interact with Video.js users through our official community channels and development platforms (including on Discord and GitHub). If you choose to interact with us through these channels and platforms, we may be able to see the information that you permit the relevant channel and platform to share with third parties (such as profile information, profile picture, and username) as well as any information you post in the channel or platform or you choose to send directly to one of our channel / platform moderators.

This information is used solely for facilitating our official community channels and development platforms (including to answer your questions and requests), as well as to understand how individuals are using our free and open source HTML5 video player and improve the offering. This information is not used by us for purposes of profiling channel / platform members, tracking individuals across services or browsing sessions, or conducting advertising.

### Information Collected Through the Free and Open Source HTML5 Video Player

The Video.js HTML5 video player framework is a free and open source solution that anyone can install on their website or other applications. Video.js does not itself collect information through the HTML5 video player. However, the company or individual that integrates the Video.js HTML5 video player into their own website or application may use the video player and services they choose to integrate with it to collect information about their users. We do not receive this information, nor do we control how third parties may collect or use this information. Please refer to the privacy notice of the company or individual with which you are interacting to learn more about how they may use the Video.js HTML5 video player and integrated third-party services to collect information about you.

For example, our website hosting provider may collect standard technical website visitor information on our behalf.

In addition, we rely on several third-party sponsors (identified on our homepage) to support the Video.js open source project. These third parties may process information on our behalf or have access to information while performing services for the project. These third-party sponsors do not use the information described in the sections above for their own business or commercial purposes. Please note our third-party sponsors may change over time and, when they do, the new third-party sponsors will be listed on our homepage.

We may also from time to time change the governing structure of the open-source project, including by changing the sponsorship structure or the legal vehicle within which the open-source project is maintained. If we do so, we may need to disclose or transfer the information described in the sections above to necessary third parties in connection with the change in governing structure.

We may also disclose the information described in the sections above to third parties, such as legal advisors and law enforcement:

- in connection with the establishment, exercise, or defense of legal claims;
- to comply with laws or to respond to lawful requests and legal process;
- to protect our rights and property and the rights and property of others, including to enforce our license terms;
- to detect, suppress, or prevent fraud;
- to protect the health and safety of us or others; or
- as otherwise required by applicable law.

## Data Retention

Server logs containing technical information may be retained for security and operational purposes for a reasonable period, typically not exceeding 12 months.

## Children's Data

Our services are not directed to, and we do not intend to, or knowingly, collect or solicit data from children under the age of 13. If an individual is under the age of 13, they should not use our services or otherwise provide us with any data either directly or by other means. If a child under the age of 13 has provided data to us, we encourage the child's parent or guardian to contact us to request that we remove the data from our systems. If we learn that any data we collect has been provided by a child under the age of 13, we will promptly delete that data.

Our documentation may link to code repositories, issue trackers, and other development resources or other third-party websites, platforms or services. Third-party websites and other services may also reference or link to our website and services. This Privacy Notice does not apply to any data practices of these third-party repositories, issue trackers, development resources, websites, platforms or other services.
